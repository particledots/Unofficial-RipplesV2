UNOFFICIAL RIPPLES V2  (AU + VST3)  -  how to install

1. Unzip the download. You get a folder called "Unofficial-RipplesV2-macOS".
2. Open Terminal and paste this (change the path if you unzipped somewhere else). It clears the "downloaded from the internet" block:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/Unofficial-RipplesV2-macOS/*
3. Copy  "Unofficial Ripples V2.component"  into  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   Copy  "Unofficial Ripples V2.vst3"       into  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   (In Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., paste the path.)
   This is a NEW plug-in with its own ID. It does not replace "Unofficial Ripples" (the first one), so both can be installed.
   If you installed an earlier build of V2, delete its .component and .vst3 first.
4. In Logic: Settings > Plug-in Manager. Find "particledots: Unofficial Ripples V2" and click Reset & Rescan Selection
   (or rescan all). Put it on an audio or instrument track as an effect.
5. Using the sidechain (FM, or a second audio input): put the plug-in on a track, then use the "Side Chain" menu in the plug-in
   window's header and pick a TRACK (for example your drum track). Choosing "input 1/2" does nothing; it must be a track.
   Then on the panel: FM SOURCE = SIDECHAIN and raise FM AMOUNT, or raise SIDECHAIN IN to hear that track through the filter.
   The SIDECHAIN LEVEL bar on the panel moves when the sidechain is really connected.
6. No sidechain at all? Use FM SOURCE = PING ENV. and click FIRE PING, or ENVELOPE (follows the plug-in's own input), or OWN INPUT.
7. The plug-in reports about 31 samples of delay to Logic (its oversampling filters).
